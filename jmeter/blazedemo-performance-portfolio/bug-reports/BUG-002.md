## What's Wrong (Summary)
When many people try to buy a flight at the same time, the payment step starts taking too long to respond. Past a certain number of people using the site at once, it crosses the point where the wait time becomes unacceptable.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Simulate a growing number of people using the site at the same time — starting from 0 and building up to 200 people over about 10 minutes.
2. Have each simulated person go through the full booking process, ending with the payment step.
3. Watch how long the payment step takes to respond as more people join.

## What Should Happen
The payment step should respond in under 2 seconds, no matter how many people are using the site at once (up to 200).

## What Actually Happens
- Around 160–165 people using the site at once, the payment step starts taking longer than 2 seconds for some users.
- At 200 people, about 5 out of 100 payment attempts were too slow, and the slowest ones were taking over 2.2 seconds.

## Why This Matters
This tells us the payment step is the weak point of the site under heavy traffic. If this were a real business, busy periods (like holidays) could cause real customers to experience slow or failed checkouts.
